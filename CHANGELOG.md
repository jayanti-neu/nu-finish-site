## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Automated video processing workflow for compressing and trimming source MOV files into web-ready desktop MP4 and mobile WebP assets

### Changed

- Refined homepage content and hierarchy to better emphasize Nu Finish's collision repair mission and customer process
- Reworked hero section with improved headline flow, clearer visual treatment, and a static building image background

- Upgraded owner spotlight messaging to a more professional, customer-relevant tone
- Updated footer messaging to focus on the company's mission
- Improved gallery interactions with clearer click affordance and animated lightbox transitions
- Reordered navigation to better match page flow and section placement
- Added adaptive styling for user light/dark mode preferences
