# Nu‑Finish Auto Collision — Content Research Plan & Checklist

A practical, step‑by‑step plan to collect the text, images, and brand assets needed to launch the website.

---

## 0) Timeline at a Glance (1–2 weeks)

* **Day 0:** Prep (set up folders, draft questionnaires, make shot list)
* **Day 1:** Owner meeting (60–90 min) — discovery + asset intake
* **Day 2–3:** On‑site content capture (photos/video, b‑roll, before/after)
* **Day 3–4:** Draft sitemap + style tiles (3 quick visual directions)
* **Day 5:** First content packet to owner (copy outline + image selects)
* **Day 6–7:** Revisions + final approvals

**Primary deliverables:** sitemap, copy outline per page, style tile (colors/type), photo set, asset library (logos, fonts), approvals log.

---

## 1) Pre‑Visit Prep (Owner Doesn’t Need to Do Anything Yet)

* **Create a folder structure**

  * `/01_Assets_brand` (logos, fonts, colors, past flyers)
  * `/02_Images_raw` (original photos) → `/02_Images_selects` (web‑ready)
  * `/03_Copy` (intake notes, quotes, services details)
  * `/04_Deliverables` (sitemap, style tiles, drafts)
* **Light desk research (30–45 min)**

  * Google Business Profile, Facebook page, existing mentions/reviews
  * Note common keywords (e.g., “collision repair Oakland MD”, “free estimates”)
* **Prepare documents** (print or digital)

  * Owner **Discovery Questionnaire** (below)
  * **Shot List** (below)
  * **Photo/Video Release** (template in Appendix)
  * **Content Matrix** (spreadsheet headers below)

---

## 2) Owner Meeting Agenda (60–90 min)

**Goal:** confirm business goals, gather brand inputs, request assets, and align on voice & visual style.

### A. Business Goals & Audience

* What are the top **3 outcomes** you want from the site? (calls, estimate requests, directions, recruiting?)
* Primary customers? (insurance claims, walk‑ins, commercial fleets)
* Geographic **service area** (towns/zip codes) and **response time** expectations.

### B. Brand & Visual Preferences

* **Adjectives** (e.g., honest, fast, premium, family‑owned)
* **Colors** you like/avoid; do you have a logo/palette already?
* **Type style** preference (tough/industrial vs. clean/modern)
* 2–3 **competitor** sites you like/dislike (and why)

### C. Services, Differentiators & Proof

* Services list (collision repair, paint/refinish, frame, ADAS calibration, claims help)
* What makes you **different**? (turnaround, OEM procedures, lifetime warranty, I‑CAR/ASE certs)
* **Certifications & memberships** (I‑CAR, ASE, OEM approvals)
* **Insurance partners** and process summary

### D. Operations & Contact

* **Hours**, holiday hours, emergency/towing partners
* Preferred **call to action** (call, form, visit) and where to route inquiries (email/phone)
* Estimate workflow (free? how to request? what photos are needed?)

### E. Assets & Permissions

* Request **logos** (AI/EPS/SVG/PNG), any **brand guidelines**, and **fonts**
* Ask permission for **photos/videos** on site & social; discuss plate/face blurring if needed
* Get ok to **feature testimonials** (first name + last initial, town)

**Wrap‑up:** confirm **approver** and turnaround time for review (e.g., 48 hr); schedule on‑site shoot.

---

## 3) On‑Site Content Capture

### Photo Shot List (prioritize horizontal 16:9 + some 4:3)

* Exterior: storefront, signage, lot with cars, entrance
* Interior: reception, bays, paint booth, tools, frame machine
* People: owner/head tech portraits, team group (with consent)
* Process: inspection, masking, color‑matching, spraying, curing
* **Before/After**: 3–5 vehicles (consistent angles; blur plates if needed)

**Specs:** 3000–4000 px longest edge; keep originals; export web selects \~150–250 KB WebP.

### B‑roll / Short Clips (optional)

* Walk‑through of shop, spray pass, polishing, alignment machine in action (5–10 sec clips)

### Audio/Quotes

* Record 2–3 **short quotes** from owner/foreman (phone is fine):

  * “Our promise to customers is…”
  * “We follow OEM procedures because…”

---

## 4) Asset Request List (send after meeting)

* Logo: **vector** (AI/EPS/SVG) + PNG on transparent
* Brand colors (HEX if available) and any typography preferences
* Past marketing materials (flyers, invoices, yard sign)
* Certifications: images of plaques/certificates
* Insurance partner logos (permission to display)
* 3–5 recent **reviews/testimonials** we can quote
* Any **warranty/guarantee** language

---

## 5) Copy Outline by Page (Owner can mark up)

### Home

* Headline: what you do + where (e.g., “Collision Repair in Oakland, MD — Free Estimates”)
* 3 value bullets (turnaround, OEM procedures, insurance help)
* Primary CTA (Call now / Request estimate)
* Trust row: certifications, years in business, reviews

### Services

* 5–7 services with 2–3 sentences each + 1 photo
* Short “How we work” section (inspection → estimate → repair → delivery)

### About

* Brief story, years, team intro, shop photo, certifications

### Gallery

* 12–20 photos, grouped before/after; captions optional

### Contact / Estimate

* NAP (Name, Address, Phone) exactly as Google Business Profile
* Hours and map embed; form or instructions for estimate photos

---

## 6) Brand Direction (Quick Style Tiles)

Create **3 simple tiles** to choose from:

* **Tile A:** Bold industrial (dark gray, safety yellow accent, condensed headline)
* **Tile B:** Clean modern (white, navy, blue accent, geometric sans)
* **Tile C:** Heritage shop (cream, deep red, slab serif headings)

**Contrast:** verify AA (≥ 4.5:1); adjust as needed.

---

## 7) Local SEO Essentials

* Confirm **canonical NAP** formatting (exact punctuation)
* Service area towns list; add to footer or About
* Page titles/meta descriptions; Open Graph image (1200×630)
* **Schema:** `AutoBodyShop` JSON‑LD with openingHours
* Ask for access to **Google Business Profile** (to sync hours/photos)

---

## 8) Content Matrix (Working Sheet)

Create a spreadsheet with columns:
`Page | Section | Owner | Source (new/existing) | Asset Needed | Status (Not Started/In Progress/Needs Review/Approved) | Due | Notes`

Use it to track every paragraph, photo, and CTA from intake → approval.

---

## 9) Approvals & Change Control

* One **approver** named; two revision rounds included in scope
* Approvals captured via email or signed PDF of the content packet
* Late asset risk: if photos delayed > 7 days, launch with temporary stock placeholders (flagged for swap)

---

## Appendix A — Owner Discovery Questionnaire (leave‑behind)

1. What should a first‑time visitor do on your site? (primary CTA)
2. Top 5 services (ranked) + any you **don’t** offer
3. What do customers love most about working with you? (quotes welcome)
4. Warranty or guarantee details
5. Turnaround time expectations
6. Insurance process in 2–3 sentences
7. Towns/areas you serve
8. Any promotions/seasonal notes
9. Colors/typefaces you prefer/avoid; attach logos
10. Who approves content and images?

---

## Appendix B — Photo/Video Release (one‑liner template)

“I grant Nu‑Finish Auto Collision and its contractors permission to photograph/record me/my vehicle for marketing use on the website and social media, royalty‑free.”
(Signature/Printed Name/Date)

---

## Appendix C — File Naming & Delivery

* **Logos:** `nufinish-logo-primary.svg`, `nufinish-logo-reversed.svg`
* **Photos:** `YYYYMMDD-location-subject-##.jpg` (e.g., `20250916-shop-exterior-01.jpg`)
* **Copy:** `home-hero-v1.md`, `services-frame-repair.md`
* Deliver via shared folder; keep originals + web exports.

---

## Success Criteria (Definition of Ready for Build)

* Approved sitemap & copy outline
* Chosen style tile with color/typography
* 12–20 approved photos + 1 OG image
* Logo SVG + HEX colors
* Confirmed NAP, hours, CTAs, and contact routing
